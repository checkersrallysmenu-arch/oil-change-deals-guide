Jiffy Lube Oil Change Coupons

Why Jiffy Lube deals change by zip code, and what is running right now. Updated October 21, 2026.

Current Jiffy Lube deals

$30 off in many regions, into November 19, 2026 Franchise groups across the country are running $30 off coupons, with many current sets expiring November 19, 2026. The exact code and date depend on which group runs your local store.
50% off conventional in select regions A few regions, Oregon and Southwest Washington among them, run a 50% off deal on a conventional oil change instead. Regional offers like this are the reason two drivers in different states can pay completely different prices on the same day.

Start at Jiffy Lube's official coupon page and enter your zip. The page shows the coupon set for your store's group, with the printed expiry date on each coupon.

Why the zip code matters so much

Jiffy Lube stores belong to regional franchise groups, and each group publishes its own coupons on its own schedule. One group's $30 coupon might run to November 19 while the group in the next state ends its set a week earlier. Coupon roundups that ignore this end up listing codes your store cannot accept, so always pull the coupon for your own zip before you drive out.
