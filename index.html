Paying Less for an Oil Change, Without the Guesswork

Verified coupons, real prices and store level details for America's biggest oil change chains.

Why prices confuse everyone

Ask five drivers what an oil change costs and you will get five different answers. The reason is simple: most chains are franchises, so one Jiffy Lube can charge a very different price from another across town, and almost nobody pays the sticker price because coupons run all year. The team behind Oil Change Coupon Codes spends its time closing that information gap, checking official brand coupon pages and recording what each offer is really worth, where it works, and when it expires.

Firestone Complete Auto Care Famous for the $19.99 Firestone oil change, a deal drivers have chased for years. Availability varies, so checking before you drive over matters.
Take 5 Oil Change The five minute, stay-in-your-car chain. Its standing offer is $15 off through a text or email coupon, and the details are gathered on the main coupon site.

Three habits that save money

First, never book before checking the brand's current coupons, because the same service can cost $25 less with a code that takes ten seconds to find. Second, match the coupon to your store, since franchise groups honor different codes. Third, check the expiry date, because oil change coupons rotate roughly every month and last month's screenshot will not scan.
