How to Pay Less for Your Next Oil Change

The current deals at the four big chains, where to get them, and the habits that keep the bill down. Updated October 20, 2026.

Why nobody pays the same price

Ask five drivers what an oil change costs and you will get five answers. Most chains are franchises, so prices change from one town to the next, and almost nobody pays the sticker price, because coupons run all year. The trick is knowing which deal your own store is running this month, and that changes more often than most people realize.

Three habits that save money

First, never book before checking the current coupons, because the same service can cost $30 less with a deal that takes ten seconds to find. Second, match the coupon to your store, since franchise groups honor different offers. Third, read the expiry date, because these deals rotate roughly every month and last month's screenshot will not scan.
