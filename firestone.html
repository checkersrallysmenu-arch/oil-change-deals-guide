Good to know before you book

Firestone is a full service auto care shop rather than a quick lube, so oil changes are booked like other services and can be paired with a tire rotation or inspection in the same visit. Posted prices typically run around $50 to $70 for conventional and $95 to $115 for full synthetic before offers, and the $15 off coupon applies on top of whichever service you choose.

Questions drivers ask

Is the $19.99 special for synthetic oil?
No. The special covers a basic conventional service at participating stores. Synthetic services cost more, and the $15 off offer is the one to use there.

Do I need an appointment?
Firestone takes walk ins, but booking ahead is the safer move, especially on weekends when the bays fill with tire and brake work.
